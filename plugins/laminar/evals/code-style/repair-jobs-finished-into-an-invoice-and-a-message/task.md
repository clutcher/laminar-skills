Finishing a repair job in this bicycle repair workshop service now also bills the customer and tells them:

- When a mechanic finishes a repair job, the request carries the minutes of labour spent and the parts used, each by part number and quantity. Finishing a job that is not open is rejected with a reason the caller can show.
- The customer gets an invoice for the job: 1 line for the labour, billed at 48.00 per hour for each started quarter hour, and 1 line per part used, at the unit price of the parts catalogue. The invoice is saved.
- The customer gets a message that the bike is ready to collect, with the invoice total.
- Keep the existing finish endpoint, and write the tests you consider right.
