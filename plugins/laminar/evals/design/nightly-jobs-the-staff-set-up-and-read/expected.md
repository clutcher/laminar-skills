## Level 2

- The job, its run and its retry setting are created classes marked `represents` `domain object` in the domain of the module `nightly`. The person who uses this part of the application is a librarian who sets up jobs and reads their runs, so a job, a run and a retry are words of that person's work, also where the domain is technical. The job is named by the word for the whole thing, such as `Job`. The word nightly, which every job of the plan shares, stands in a field of the job, such as the time of the night at which it runs, or in the name of its module, and never in the name of the job. No ending word of the name is graded.
- The job carries `madeWhen` naming the librarian who sets it up, such as "a librarian sets up a job". The retry setting carries `partOf` naming the job, and the job holds it as a field. The run carries `madeWhen` naming a run of a job that a librarian set up, such as "a job that a librarian set up reaches its time", or `partOf` naming the job where the job holds its runs. Each class with `partOf` stands nested in its whole, in a subfolder named for its whole that holds the whole too, or in a folder that holds only the whole and its parts. It never stands in a folder with a class that is neither its whole nor a part of it.
- The rule whether a run of a job tries again after a failed attempt, which reads the retry setting of the job and the attempts of the run, stands in a class marked `work`, or as a method that takes the data of the other only as plain values on the retry setting, on the run, or on the job that holds the retry setting, such as a method of the retry setting that takes the count of attempts so far. The line fails where a method of the run takes the retry setting or the job, or a method of the job or of the retry setting takes the run. This place is a rule of the skill, narrower than the book.
- The kinds of work may be a map field of the runner, or of the class that starts a run, built by the wiring from the classes of the kinds and handed to it. This form passes, and it is not the only form that passes. The key of such a map is the name of the kind, and the lookup by that name is a method of that class; or the key is an enum of the kinds, and the lookup by the name is a method of that enum. A class marked `work`, other than a record among the domain objects, that holds the kinds of work and looks 1 up by its name also passes, whatever its name. The line fails only where a type marked `domain object`, or a record among the domain objects, holds only the collection of the kinds of work and looks 1 up in it. A missing name fails with `UnknownKindOfWorkException`.
- Every created class outside a test file, a failure type and the `config` zone carries `represents`. Each kind of work is marked `work`, or `translator` where it stands in the `out` zone.
- At `ready`, the design check prints no line of `kind-does-not-fit` on any class of the model. It prints no line of `type-holds-only-a-collection` on the job, the run, the retry setting or any other class marked `domain object`.
